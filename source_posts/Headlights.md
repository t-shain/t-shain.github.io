# A review of Headlights by Alex G

Is there emotional learning available to us in moments so full of feeling the human mind loses its own ability to communicate to itself? I think so. Like the title, Alex Gs Headlights flashes us with moments of emotional learning both complex and clarifying. The album feels like flipping through a book you read last week. Eating the same sandwich at a new place. Taking a look through a mirror that's been there for years and being relieved you have seen someone else. 

> Im gonna put that football way up in the sky

Smoke and mirrors dance throughout each song, while he wields these long, often slowly paced melodies in textured atmospheres, and leaving us to consider our natural thoughts for once. Its an album that is a partner  through the viewers own emotional journey. In contradiction to many of Alex Gs separate works it feels almost entirely unselfish as well. Taking risks in ways that seem like those a man with wisdom of the musical experience would take. 

>Portraits fall, im not scared
