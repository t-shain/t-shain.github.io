## Step One 

Can the internet work outside of the bounds imposed by the modern era? Outside of money and influence? It wants to be a collection of messages and artifacts of humanity, and in most cases it is. Besides the bots that populate all the dead crevices, even the monolithic drug companies websites tell stories about what we are. FAQs examine our rationality, the contact page maps out the links in our communication.

**We interlinked our thoughts with the internet and no matter the economics of our present moment, those links remain important.** 

## Step Two 

I would like to help build and maintain this system, become a doctor to its afflictions. But in order to do so I must participate in the money and influence of long past institutions. No matter the current ambitions of these place, they are sinking ships. Climate change, capitalism, social upheaval and reformation, dark money private equity death-murder buyouts, they will sink these ships. 

I feel left without choice and like most of the people in my life *I dont know what to do about it*. It always feels corny to say things like "vote" or "find a passion to lose yourself in". Making art feels the most spiritually healing, but not enough to convince the agents of the private equity death-murder buyouts, its only prescription to stop the hearts own bleeding. Existentialist love a  rock and hard place, they tell us how wonderful life can be to live in spite of proposed beliefs and restrictions. To live for each other in this world of pain can be especially beautiful, I can should then connect people and ideas, in order to stop pain and create communal virtue. 

## Step Three

I want to use this site to simply explain my mind to myself. I am sentimental at heart and even the bits running through this hold signifiants to me. So I will write, and hopefully the steps I have taken from now into perpetuity will reverberate into space until virtue is found on earth.
