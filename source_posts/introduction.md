
## Step One 

Can the internet work outside of the bounds imposed by the modern era? Outside of money and influence? It wants to be a collection of messages and artifacts of humanity, and in most cases it is. Besides the bots that populate all the dead crevices, even the monolithic drug companies websites tell stories about what we are. FAQs examine our rationality, the contact page maps out the links in our communication.

**We interlinked our thoughts with the internet and no matter the economics of our present moment, those links remain important.**
