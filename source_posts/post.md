# Kitten Mitten Cup Rules

1. Top 100ish players are banned from being on anyones team. This is called the boneyard
2. The league is a keeper league 
3. It is done in yahoo fantasy.

Format of a single team 

| Kind             | Number Starting | Max Number on roster |
| ---------------- | --------------- | -------------------- |
| Centers `C`      | 2               | 4                    |
| Right Wings `RW` | 2               | 4                    |
| Left Wings `LW`  | 2               | 4                    |
| Defense `D`      | 4               | 6                    |
| Goalie `G`       | 1               | 2                    |

5 players are kept between seasons. With a **total roster size** of 20. The boneyard will be updated after a season is over and before the 5 players that are kept are decided. 

If a new player that was previously on a roster is now in the boneyard that player is dropped but the manager receives an extra pick in the following years draft.
